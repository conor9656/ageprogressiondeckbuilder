# Doctrines — full draft pool

Faith / Missionary-triggered. Pay out **only if you win** this settlement (unless noted).  
Each Doctrine fires at most once per settlement when a Missionary plays it. Missionary tier scales **numeric** rewards.

Modes: **Flat econ** · **Combat buff (next fights)** · **Scaling** · **Occupy/rule** · **Retrigger**

Doctrines are where **run snowballs** live — keep combat-forward Doctrines uncommon/rare and capped.

Strategies: S5 Faith primary; also S6 Science, econ, bosses.

**OP red flags:** uncapped permanent ×Might; retrigger-all without Missionary cost; Science% of damage with no diminishing return into era 6+.

## Balance watchlist

- **Zeal (D11) + Banner of Faith (D19) + Apostolic Blessing (D24)** can stack Might% — prefer non-stacking rule: Might% buffs take **max**, not sum, unless from different durations.
- **Scriptorium (D02)** cap is mandatory once Capitals deal huge damage.
- **Holy War (D22)** with 3 Missionaries and 4 Doctrines is lottery mode — OK if Missionary slots are expensive.
- Scaling Doctrines should be **attractive early** (cheap Faith pack weight) so players feel growth; commons D09/D10 are the teaching tools.
