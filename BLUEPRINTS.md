# Blueprints — full draft pool

Gold / Builder-triggered. Each fires **at most once per settlement** (unless a card says otherwise).  
Fight-local by default; a few **permanently scale** when fired. Builder tier multiplies **numeric** fields (~+50–100% per tier).

Modes: **Flat** (this fight) · **Burst** (next Assault only) · **Utility** · **Scaling** (run-long growth when used) · **Rule** (walls, assaults, draws)

Strategies: S1–S6 as in `CONTENT_CATALOG.md`.

**OP red flags:** +Assault commons; permanent ×Mom that stacks every fight with no cap; “all Blueprints retrigger” without cost.

## Balance watchlist

- **Aqueduct (B23)** alone can turn losses into wins — cost high Gold and low pack weight.
- **Royal Laboratory (B26)** + Magazine (B15) + Momentum Policies: monitor total Mom; Forge should stay unit-Might-only.
- Scaling Blueprints (B09, B10, B21, B22, B29) must show `Currently` and caps in UI.
- Builder tier scaling on already-capped blues: multiply the **post-cap numeric**, or multiply before cap? Prefer **multiply effect, still respect cap** so Engineer doesn’t break caps.
