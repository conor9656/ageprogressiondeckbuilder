# Card design modes — Ages modifiers

Inspiration from deck-builder scoring design (flat additives, multipliers, growth over a run). **No copied card text** — patterns only, applied to Ages.

Damage is always **Might × Momentum** (then settlement modifiers). So:

| Lever | Feels like | Careful? |
|---|---|---|---|
| +Might | Additive “chips” | Safest common tool |
| +Momentum | Additive “mult” | Stronger per point than Might |
| ×Might / ×Momentum | Multiplicative mult | Rare; ×Mom especially |
| Economy / draws / slots | Utility & shop fuel | Needed so packs stay fun |
| Scaling (“Currently: X”) | Growth jokers | Need **caps** or **once-per-settlement** growth |

## Where each mode belongs

| System | Best for | Weak at |
|---|---|---|
| **Policies** (`POLICIES.md`) | Flat, conditional, slow scalers, passive econ | One-fight bursts |
| **Blueprints** (`BLUEPRINTS.md`) | This-settlement bursts, wall rules, draw, grow-on-fire | Permanent run ×Mom without caps |
| **Doctrines** (`DOCTRINES.md`) | Victory pay, next-fight buffs, Faith snowballs | Raw every-Assault damage |

## Flat vs scaling (design rule)

- **Flat:** readable, draftable, good commons. Power is the number.
- **Scaling:** must show current value; prefer growth on **settlement win** or **once per settlement when fired**, not every Assault.
- **Hard caps** on almost all scalers (listed per card).
